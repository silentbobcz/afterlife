# AFTERLIFE · the pitch

## Short (one line)

> Keep your chrome on ice, jack in only what you load to the deck. A crash-safe mod manager for Cyberpunk 2077.

## Medium (a paragraph)

> Wake up, choom. Your mod folder's a flatlined mess and the game's one bad archive away from dying on the loading screen. AFTERLIFE keeps every mod on ice in its own library and only jacks into Night City what you load to the deck. Profiles for every run, a switch for every collection, conflicts you can actually read, and a journal that finishes the job or rolls you back if the power dies mid-install. No telemetry, no corpo strings. Free, open source, MIT. Preem.

## Long (the full spiel)

**Wake up, choom. We got a mod folder to burn.**

You know the drill. Fifty mods, three frameworks, a redscript that hates your TweakXL, and one patch from CDPR turns the whole stack into a smoking cyberdeck. Somewhere in `archive/pc/mod` there's a file nobody remembers installing. Vortex says one thing, your folder says another, and the game says *nothing*, because it crashed.

**AFTERLIFE is the bar where legends go to rest, and where your mods wait their turn.**

- **The library is the fixer's back room.** Every mod gets checked at the door: paths validated, layout read, every file hashed. Nothing touches Night City until you say so.
- **Load to the deck.** Hit *Apply*, see exactly what goes in and what comes out, then jack it in. Every write is temp file → hash → journal → rename → verify. Power cut mid-run? Next boot it finishes the job or puts everything back. No half-installed chrome, ever.
- **Profiles are your loadouts.** Street-kid playthrough, photo-mode tourist, vanilla for the achievements, *Mods off* when something smells wrong. Swap between them without reinstalling a byte.
- **Core mods get their own corner.** CET, RED4ext, redscript, ArchiveXL, TweakXL, Codeware and friends, versions in the header, tracked as requirements. Collections never mess with them.
- **Requirements without the guesswork.** Read from the files and from the Nexus page. Missing something? It glows pink and *Open page* takes you right to it.
- **Conflicts you can read.** Two mods, one file, you pick the winner. One file or all of them. Load order by drag and drop.
- **Nexus wired in.** *Mod Manager Download* drops straight into the library. Updates checked at start and every hour with one request, so Nexus doesn't get mad. Premium? *Update all* and done.
- **Collections, minus the panic.** Paste a link, see what you've got and what's missing, pause and resume the download, flip the whole collection on or off.
- **REDmod and FOMOD handled.** REDmods deployed before launch, FOMOD installers as a proper wizard. Scripts in archives never run.
- **Scan manual mods.** Found something you installed by hand back in 2023? AFTERLIFE shows it and can move it into the library. Always reversible.

**No telemetry. No ads. No self-updater phoning home. No corpo strings attached.**
Talks to Nexus Mods only, and only when you ask it to. Your API key stays encrypted on your machine.

Free. Open source. MIT. Built for Windows by a fan, for fans.
Not approved or endorsed by CD PROJEKT RED. Just a choom with a mod list that got out of hand.

*See you in the Afterlife.*
